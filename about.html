Our story

Why we work this way.

Many companies pass a project between separate designers, developers and testers. Every handoff means someone has to learn your project again, and that is where time gets lost.

We keep everything in one small team. You talk directly to the people doing the work, you see progress at every stage, and your feedback shapes the result before it is finished.

After launch we stay reachable for the fixes, updates and small improvements that keep your product working as your business changes.
