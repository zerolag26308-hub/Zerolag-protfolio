Services

Everything it takes to go from zero to launch.

Web, mobile, UI/UX, video, AI, software engineering, digital marketing and white-label delivery — eight disciplines under one roof, run by people who build the whole stack instead of handing pieces off between specialists.

01 — Service

Web Development

From custom-built marketing sites to full-stack web applications, we cover the whole spectrum of web development — custom website development, e-commerce storefronts, CMS builds on WordPress and Webflow, progressive web apps, single-page applications, landing pages and microsites, and full-stack MERN, MEAN or LAMP builds, all with performance and standards compliance baked in from the first commit.

What We Solve

  • Marketing sites that load slow and lose visitors before the first scroll
  • Online stores that make it hard to browse, checkout or manage inventory
  • CMS setups that fight the team trying to update them

Deliverables

  • A fully responsive, production-ready website or web app
  • A CMS or e-commerce platform your team can actually manage
  • Clean, documented, framework-appropriate code across the stack

Process: technical scoping, architecture and component planning, build in staged milestones, QA across browsers and devices, launch.

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • React
  • Next.js
  • WordPress

02 — Service

Mobile App Development

We build native and cross-platform mobile apps that feel like they belong on the device they're running on — native Swift for iOS, native Kotlin for Android, and React Native or Flutter where a shared codebase makes more sense, backed by app store optimization so the app gets found once it ships.

What We Solve

  • Needing both iOS and Android without doubling the budget
  • Native capabilities — camera, push, biometrics — a web app can't reach
  • Apps that launch to zero downloads because nobody can find them

Deliverables

  • A submission-ready iOS and/or Android app
  • A shared cross-platform codebase where it makes sense, native where it doesn't
  • App Store and Play Store listing, ASO and release support

Process: platform strategy, UI build and native integration, device testing, ASO and store submission.

  • Swift
  • Kotlin
  • React Native
  • Flutter

Video Editing & Production

We plan, shoot, animate and edit video content built to hold attention — promotional and corporate videos, product demos and explainer videos, motion graphics and 3D animation, and short-form content cut specifically for social feeds, all finished with the same post-production care as the rest of the build.

What We Solve

  • Social content that looks like an afterthought instead of a strategy

Deliverables

  • Motion graphics or 3D animation assets sized for their platform
  • Platform-cut social edits with captions, pacing and formats matched to each channel

Process: creative brief and script, shoot or asset sourcing, edit and motion design, revisions, platform-ready export.

AI & Machine Learning

We build custom AI solutions, machine learning models, NLP pipelines, computer vision systems and conversational AI — integrated into real product workflows, not left running as a standalone demo nobody ships. That includes predictive analytics and AI-powered automation that removes real manual work, not just a chatbot bolted onto a homepage.

What We Solve

  • AI features that look great in a demo and never reach production
  • Manual workflows that could be predicted or automated but aren't
  • Chatbots and NLP tools that misunderstand users more often than they help them

Deliverables

  • A production AI or ML feature integrated into your existing product
  • A trained, evaluated model or NLP/computer vision pipeline tuned to your data
  • A deployed chatbot or automation workflow with monitoring in place

Process: use-case scoping and data audit, model or pipeline design, integration into the product, evaluation and monitoring setup.

  • TensorFlow

What We Solve

Deliverables

  • A CI/CD pipeline with monitoring for ongoing deployments
  • Custom software, a SaaS product or a system integration built to spec

Process: architecture and data modeling, build in staged milestones, integration and load testing, CI/CD and deployment setup.

White-Label Services
