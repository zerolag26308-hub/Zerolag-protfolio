Process

A calmer, faster way to build.

We don't jump straight to design. Every project moves through the same sequence, with you involved at each stage, so nothing gets built on guesswork.

01

Discover

We start by understanding your business, audience and objectives before any design work begins. This stage sets the constraints and the goals everything else is measured against.

What happens

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Competitive and market review
  • Technical and content audit
  • Goals and success criteria defined
02

Strategize

With research in hand, we plan the structure, content and user experience around how your visitors actually think and make decisions.

What happens

  • Sitemap and information architecture
  • User flows for key journeys
  • Content and messaging plan
  • Scope and technical approach agreed
03

Design

We create the visual direction and interface (typography, layout, colour and motion), refined through your feedback before production code is written.

What happens

  • Visual direction and moodboard
  • Wireframes and page layouts
  • High-fidelity desktop and mobile screens
  • Design review and revisions
04

Develop

Approved designs become a fast, responsive, standards-based product, built with clean and maintainable code from the start.

What happens

  • Semantic, accessible front-end build
  • Responsive implementation across breakpoints
  • Interactions, animation and behaviour
  • Integrations where the project needs them
05

Test

Before anything ships, we test functionality, accessibility, responsiveness and performance across real browsers and devices.

What happens

  • Cross-browser and cross-device checks
  • Accessibility and keyboard review
  • Performance and load-time audit
  • Content and link QA
06

Launch

We deploy the finished product, optimise it for search and performance, and confirm everything works in its live environment.

What happens

  • Deployment to your hosting environment
  • Final SEO and metadata checks
  • Analytics and tracking setup
  • Go-live confirmation
07

Support

Launch isn't the end of the relationship. We stay available for updates, fixes and improvements as your business evolves.

What happens

  • Post-launch monitoring
  • Security and dependency updates
  • Ongoing maintenance plans
  • Future enhancements as you grow

Ready to start the process?

Tell us what you're working on and we'll walk you through how it would apply to your project.
