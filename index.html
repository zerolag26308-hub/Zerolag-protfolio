FAQ

Common questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

How do I get started?

Fill in the quote form or email zerolag26308@gmail.com. We usually reply within one business day. You can also see how it works.

How long does a website take?

Timelines depend on scope. A focused marketing site typically takes a few weeks; larger builds with custom functionality take longer. We'll give you a realistic estimate after discovery.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work remotely with clients across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Europe, collaborating across time zones.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Redesigns are one of our core services. We audit what's there, keep what works, and rebuild the rest around modern design and performance standards.

Do you provide maintenance?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance plans covering updates, monitoring, fixes and small enhancements after launch.

Can you work with my existing branding?

Absolutely. We can design within your existing brand guidelines, or help evolve your identity as part of a broader redesign.

Do you provide hosting and deployment?

We can advise on and handle deployment to your preferred hosting provider, or manage the full setup for you if you don't have one yet.

How do payments work?

Projects are typically split into milestone-based payments, agreed before work begins. Full details are confirmed in your project proposal.

Send a short description of your project. We'll reply with any questions, a rough timeline and a quote.
