Before you ask

How long does delivery take?

Small goods leave our Portmere stockroom within two working days and arrive in three to five. Sofas and armchairs are made to order, so allow five weeks. Our two-person crew will carry them to any room you choose.

What does delivery cost?

Orders over $150 ship free. Anything under that is a flat $12, and pickup from Harbor Street is always free.

Can I return something?

Yes. Small goods can come back unused within 30 days. Seating has a 60-night trial, and if it doesn't suit, we collect it at no charge.

How does the repair promise work?

Email a photo to repairs@example.com. We will send a part, book a local upholsterer or arrange collection. It is free for five years, and fairly priced after that.
