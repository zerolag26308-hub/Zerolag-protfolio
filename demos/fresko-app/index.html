Good food from nearby kitchens, at your door.

Fresko brings the neighbourhood's favourite kitchens into one calm little app. Find dinner fast, pay cash at the door, and watch it ride over.

  • Find it by cravingSearch any dish, then narrow by cuisine or free delivery.
  • Cash on deliveryNo cards to enter. Pay your rider when the food arrives.
  • Live order trackingFollow every step from the kitchen to your front door.

This prototype is live. Tap through the phone: pick a kitchen, fill a cart, and follow the delivery.
