We work in regions, not cities, because the good bit is usually an hour past the last signpost. Pick one to see what is open there.

The sunset rule

A stay gets four questions from our scouts. Miss one and it does not appear here, however good the photographs are.

  1. Is there somewhere to sit, facing the right way, at the hour that counts?
  2. Is the owner, or someone who knows the owner by name, on site?
  3. Could a guest find breakfast within a ten minute walk?
  4. Would we pay the full nightly rate to come back?

Field notes

Twenty minutes of blue on the Java Coast

The staff at The Blue Hour turn off the terrace lamps at 6:10 every evening, and nobody tells you why. Then the pool comes up from below, the sky goes the colour of a school uniform, and the whole hotel stops talking. Book the east wing. Order the fried tempeh before the kitchen gets busy.

Dispatch 14, by our scout M. Okafor (fictional)

Pack less for the lake

The Larchwater boat takes one soft bag per person and a crate of whatever you bought at the village bakery. Everything else stays in the car park with Herr Dorn, who has never lost a suitcase.

Dispatch 13, by T. Lindqvist (fictional)

Why we like the Andaman in the wet

Mid-September rain falls for an hour after lunch and clears by three. Rates at the Teakwood Lodge drop by a fifth, and you get the long pool to yourself for the price of one short afternoon indoors.

Dispatch 12, by S. Raman (fictional)

Paris, from the wrong bank

Rue Perdue sits above a shuttered boatyard, not a boulevard. Walk the river at seven with a coffee from the corner and you will share the view with two rowers and one very serious dog.

Before you ask

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Every stay is free to cancel up to 14 days before arrival, and half refundable up to 3 days before. The owners agree to this before we list them.

What is included in the total?

The nightly rate, the stay's cleaning fee, our 7% service fee and local tax. Stays of seven nights or more get 10% off the room rate. There is nothing further to pay on arrival.

Why are some stays closed for months at a time?

Because the lake freezes or the road floods. If a stay is closed on your dates we hide it from your search rather than offer you a bad week.

Can I bring the dog?

At the Teakwood Lodge, Saltline and both Alpine Lakes stays, yes. Tell the trip desk when you reserve so the owner can leave out a bowl.
