Where the lag goes

Not shortcuts. Structure.

Most agencies slow down because they're several agencies wearing one name: a design shop subcontracting development, which subcontracts QA, each handoff adding a queue and a re-briefing. Design, engineering and every discipline in between sit on our team, working from the same brief, in parallel.

Parallel workstreams mean design and engineering move together. While a screen is still being refined, the components behind it are already being built. Reusable design and development systems mean we extend what already works instead of reinventing a component library or deployment pipeline on every project.

And when you have a question, you ask the person who can actually answer it. That's what removes the lag.
