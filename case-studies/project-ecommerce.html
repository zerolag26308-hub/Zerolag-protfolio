E-commerce · Concept

Aster & Co.

A home goods storefront built to make browsing, comparing and buying feel simple — especially on a phone, where most shopping happens.

Industry
E-commerce / Home Goods
Services
E-commerce Development, Web Design
Technology
WordPress, WooCommerce, MySQL
Timeline
Concept build

This is a self-initiated concept project. Aster & Co. is a fictional brand used to demonstrate our process; no client results are implied.

Overview

Aster & Co. is a concept home goods brand needing a storefront that felt as considered as its products, without sacrificing the practical mechanics of a good e-commerce experience: fast browsing, clear pricing and an easy checkout.

Challenge

Many small e-commerce sites either lean too hard into decorative design at the cost of usability, or default to a generic storefront template that undersells the product. Aster needed both a distinct visual identity and a genuinely smooth path from browsing to checkout.

Strategy

We treated product photography and pricing clarity as the hierarchy anchors on every page, keeping decorative elements secondary so nothing competes with the products themselves or slows down the path to purchase.

Design

A warm, editorial product grid replaces the dense catalog-style layout common to many storefronts, with collection pages that read more like a lookbook than a spreadsheet.

Development

Built on WordPress with WooCommerce for catalog and checkout management, giving the Aster team full control over products and inventory without needing developer support for day-to-day updates.

  • Fast, filterable product browsing
  • Streamlined cart and checkout flow
  • Mobile-optimized product galleries
  • Performance-tuned image delivery
  • Inventory-ready catalog structure
  • Accessible product and checkout forms

Responsive Experience

Checkout and product galleries were designed mobile-first, with thumb-friendly tap targets and a condensed cart summary that stays visible without crowding the screen.

Outcome

The result is a storefront that feels distinctly Aster's own, with a shopping experience that stays fast and clear from the homepage through to checkout on any device.
