Corporate / Technology · Concept Project

Fathom Systems

A B2B technology company website built to explain a technical product clearly to enterprise buyers and move them toward a sales conversation.

Industry
Corporate / Technology
Services
Web Development, UI/UX Design
Technology
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Node.js, REST APIs
Timeline
Concept build

Overview

Fathom Systems is a concept B2B technology company needing a site that communicates technical credibility to enterprise buyers, speaking to both technical evaluators and non-technical decision-makers on the same buying committee.

Challenge

A dense technical offering is hard to explain well to a mixed audience — technical buyers want depth and specifics, while executives want a fast, confident summary. The site also needed strong trust signals and a clear, low-friction path to a sales conversation.

Strategy

We separated the site into clear product, solutions and company sections, so each audience can self-select into the depth of detail they need, with a consistent path to "talk to sales" available from every section rather than a single buried contact page.

Design

A structured, confident visual language — clear grids, restrained color and precise typography — signals technical credibility without tipping into cold or impersonal, keeping the tone approachable for less technical readers.

Development

The frontend is built with semantic HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript, backed by a lightweight Node.js API layer that handles demo-request submissions and routes them into the sales team's workflow.

  • Solutions pages organized by buyer type
  • Resources and case studies section
  • Demo request flow backed by a REST API
  • Company and leadership section for trust-building
  • Scannable, executive-friendly page layouts
  • Accessible navigation and form patterns

Responsive Experience

Solutions pages and the demo request flow were designed to hold up on tablet and mobile, since a meaningful share of enterprise research happens outside a desk, without losing the structured layout that makes the content easy to scan.

Outcome

The result is a site that gives Fathom a clearer way to explain a technical product to a mixed buying committee, with a direct path to a sales conversation from any page. As a concept project, there are no live metrics to report — this case study is intended to demonstrate our approach for a business in this space.
