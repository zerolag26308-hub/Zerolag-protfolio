Healthcare · Concept Project

Cedarview Clinic

A clinic website designed to help patients find the right care and book an appointment quickly — without feeling cold or clinical.

Industry
Healthcare / Medical Clinic
Services
UI/UX Design, Website Design
Technology
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
Timeline
Concept build

Overview

Cedarview Clinic is a concept healthcare practice needing a site that feels trustworthy and calming rather than clinical, while making it fast for patients — including less tech-savvy ones — to find the right service and book an appointment.

Challenge

Patients arriving at a clinic site are often anxious or in a hurry to find the right service and get in touch. The site needed to explain services and providers clearly, keep contact and booking within easy reach from any page, and remain usable for an older or less tech-savvy audience without feeling patronizing.

Strategy

We built the information architecture around how patients actually search — by symptom, service or provider — and kept appointment and phone-call actions visible from the homepage through every service and provider page, rather than buried on a single contact page.

Design

A calm, uncluttered visual language — soft color palette, generous whitespace and larger-than-default type — replaces the sterile, corporate feel common to clinic sites, while large touch targets and simple, predictable navigation keep the experience easy for every age group.

Development

Built with semantic HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript, the site uses accessible markup and form patterns throughout, including a client-side validated appointment request form that keeps the concept build lightweight and dependency-free.

  • Clear service and provider pages
  • Appointment request form with validation
  • Prominent click-to-call and directions
  • Accessible typography and color contrast
  • Location, hours and insurance information
  • Simple, predictable navigation for all ages

Responsive Experience

Designed mobile-first, since many patients search for a clinic and book from their phone — appointment and call actions stay within thumb's reach on every screen size.

Outcome

The result is a site that feels reassuring rather than clinical, with a clear path from any page to booking an appointment or reaching the clinic directly. As a concept project, there are no live metrics to report — this case study is intended to demonstrate our design and development approach for a business in this space.
