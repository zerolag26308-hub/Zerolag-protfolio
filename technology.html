How we choose

Matched to the problem.

A short guide to what we usually reach for, and why.

Marketing sitesSemantic HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No unnecessary framework overhead, fast by default.
Product & SaaS UIsReact and Next.js for interfaces that need state, routing and interactivity at scale.
Content-driven sitesWordPress or a custom CMS when the team behind the site needs to publish without a developer.
Online storesWooCommerce, Shopify or custom checkout flows, matched to your catalogue and payment needs.
Custom web appsNode.js/Express or PHP/Laravel backends, REST APIs and a database matched to the data model.
Ongoing operationGit-based version control, proper deployment pipelines and monitoring once a project is live.
