How We Choose

The right stack for the job, not the trend of the year.

Marketing Sites

Semantic HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript — no unnecessary framework overhead, fast by default.

Product & SaaS UIs

React and Next.js for interfaces that need state, routing and interactivity at scale.

Content-Driven Sites

WordPress or a custom CMS when the team behind the site needs to publish without a developer.

Online Stores

WooCommerce, Shopify or custom checkout flows, matched to your catalog and payment needs.

Custom Web Apps

Node.js/Express or PHP/Laravel backends, REST APIs and a database chosen to match the data model.

Ongoing Operation

Git-based version control, proper deployment pipelines and monitoring once a project is live.
