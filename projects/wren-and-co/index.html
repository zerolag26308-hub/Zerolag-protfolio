Brand identity

Identity for a boutique lifestyle label: linen, ceramics, small-batch candles.

A quiet, editorial identity built to be used by two people without a design team. Every asset on this page is the real thing: the logo is inline SVG you can download, the colours are measured for contrast, and the applications are set in the actual type.

01 — Logo system

A monogram that stands alone, a wordmark that stays quiet.

The W is drawn from four strokes inside a thin ring, with a single dot where the two peaks would meet. It reads as a bird in flight at small sizes and as a letter at large ones. The wordmark sets the name in Fraunces with an italic ampersand so it never feels corporate.

Horizontal lockupPrimary. Website header, email, invoices.
StackedSquare placements, labels.
MonogramFavicon, wax seal, embroidery.
ReversedOn Thicket, Clay or photography with a dark overlay.

Clear space

Keep a margin of x on every side, where x is the height of the ring. Nothing else sits inside it: no type, no edges, no photography.

Minimum size

Below these sizes the tagline drops and the stroke of the W closes up. Use the monogram alone when the width is under 120 px.

02 — Colour

Two colours with a name, and a ramp of linen between them.

Thicket carries the brand; Clay is the only accent and is used sparingly, for one action or one word on a page. Everything else is the neutral ramp, which is warm enough to sit next to undyed fabric and unglazed clay without looking grey.

Text on colour

Contrast ratios are calculated from the hex values above as the page loads, so a change to the palette shows up here immediately. AA requires 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text.

03 — Typography

Fraunces for the voice, Figtree for the instructions.

Fraunces is a soft, slightly wonky serif that takes well to large sizes and italics. Figtree does the work underneath: prices, care labels, shipping notes. One weight of each for most things, so the system stays simple to use.

04 — Voice & tone

Written like a note from the person who made it.

Wren & Co speaks plainly about materials, time and care. It never sells a lifestyle, never exclaims, and never pretends an object will change your life. Four principles, each with the same copy written both ways.

One

Say less, mean it

Short sentences. One idea per line. If a word is doing no work, cut it.

DoProduct descriptionOak serving board. Oiled, not varnished, so it can be sanded back and oiled again. 38 × 22 cm.
Don'tProduct descriptionElevate your hosting game with our stunning, artisanal oak serving board, lovingly crafted to make every gathering unforgettable!

Two

Describe the object, not the lifestyle

Material, origin, dimensions, how it ages. Let the reader decide what it means for them.

DoInstagram captionThe Fen candle, back on the bench this week. Cedar, dry grass, a little smoke. Forty at a time.
Don'tInstagram captionCosy season is HERE and we are obsessed. Light the Fen, pour a glass, and let the self-care begin ✨🕯️

Three

Honest about limits

Small batches run out. Say so, say when, and don't dress it up as scarcity marketing.

DoOut-of-stock noticeSold out. The next batch is poured on 14 November and ships the week after. Leave your email and we'll tell you once, when it's ready.
Don'tOut-of-stock noticeGONE! Our bestseller flew off the shelves. Join the waitlist NOW before you miss out again!

Four

Warm, never cute

Friendly like a good shopkeeper, not like a mascot. No puns in transactional messages.

DoOrder email subjectYour order is on its way — Wren & Co, no. 2184
Don'tOrder email subjectWren-derful news!! Your goodies have left the nest 🐦📦
