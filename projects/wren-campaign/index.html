The idea and the three messaging pillars

One line that everything hangs off, three things we are allowed to say, and the voice rules that keep twelve posts across four channels sounding like one brand.

Campaign line

Make room for fewer, better things.

The relaunch cuts the range from 140 products to 38. The campaign makes that the point: not a smaller shop, a clearer one. Every post shows something being used in a real room, never styled on a plinth.

1

Fewer, better

We cut the range on purpose. Say so, and say what stayed and why.

  • 38 products, each with a reason to exist
  • Every piece shown with the one thing it replaced
  • No "new arrivals" language; everything is new, so nothing is
SayWe kept the lamp that gets switched on every night.
Don't sayCurated edit of elevated essentials.
2

Made to be used

Wear, repairs and ten-year-old pieces are the proof. Show things mid-life, not box-fresh.

  • Photos in lived-in rooms, daylight, nothing styled flat
  • Repairs bench content every week
  • Customers' older pieces welcomed under the hashtag
SaySeven years in, re-waxed twice, still the bag he reaches for.
Don't sayTimeless luxury that lasts a lifetime.
3

Room to live

The payoff of owning less is space: on the floor, on the shelf, in the week.

  • Before-and-after rooms from the team's own homes
  • Short, calm video with natural sound
  • Pinterest boards organised by room, not product
SayOne good chair. The rest of the corner is yours.
Don't sayTransform your space with our collection.

UGC hashtag guideline card

The card the community team works from, and a caption checker the team uses before resharing anything to make sure the credit and tag rules are met.

#MakeRoomWithWren

We reshare photos that

  • Show a Wren piece in a real room, in use
  • Include the hashtag and tag @wrenandco
  • Are the poster's own photo, with the caption they wrote
  • Show wear, age or repairs; older pieces are the best ones

We don't reshare

  • Photos with other brands' logos in frame
  • Anything with children's faces unless the parent asked us to
  • Posts that make claims about the product we would not make
  • Anything from an account that asked not to be featured

The three kinds of photo we are asking for

Before resharing we reply on the original post asking for permission and wait for a yes. We credit the handle in the first line of our caption, keep their caption as a quote, and take it down within a day if asked.
