Production notes

A thirty-second launch film that introduces Fresko in four beats: the craving, the app, the order and the delivery. The phone is a drawn device, so every screen state is animated by hand rather than screen-recorded, which keeps type crisp at any output size.

Each beat ends on a hard transition, a circle wipe, a diagonal wipe and a rising curtain, which gives the edit a rhythm a music bed can sit on. The whole piece runs from a single timeline, so it scrubs frame-accurately and can be re-rendered at any resolution or trimmed to 15 and 6 second cutdowns.
