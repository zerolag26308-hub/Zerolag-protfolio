Aster & Co.

Digital marketing · Search strategy

A search plan built around how people shop for things that last

Six working deliverables for a small home-goods store in Portmere, Maine: what to fix on the site, which searches each page should own, what to publish over twelve weeks, how every page should be titled, how pages link to each other, and what the search engines should be told about products and the shop.

Scope
Technical audit, keyword map, content calendar, on-page templates, internal linking, structured data
Site
Shop with 8 product lines, guides section, repairs page
Horizon
12-week rollout, then quarterly review

04 / 06

On-page templates

Title, meta description and H1 patterns for each page type. Change the inputs or the pattern itself and the search snippet on the right updates as you type, with length checks against the usual display limits.

Tokens you can use in the patterns:

{product}{category}{material}{guide}{brand}{town}
Aster & Co.asterandco.example › sofas › marlow-three-seat-sofa
Page H1
  • Titles stay under 60 characters so they rarely truncate; brand goes last and is dropped first if space runs out.
  • Meta descriptions lead with the thing searched for, then the two reasons to buy here: made to order in Maine, repaired free for five years.
  • The H1 matches the title's first phrase word for word, so the page reads as the thing the result promised.
  • Collection titles name the category twice at most; product titles never repeat the category if it is already in the product name.
05 / 06

Internal-linking plan

How authority flows through the store: home to collections, collections to products, guides back into the collections they help people choose from. Hover or focus any page to see its inbound and outbound links.

Linking rules

  • R1Every product links up to its collection and sideways to two neighbours in the same collection.
  • R2Every guide links to one collection and the two products it mentions, using the product name as the link text.
  • R3Collections link to their most relevant guide in the intro copy, not just the footer.
  • R4Repairs is linked from every product page under the price, and from the footer.
  • R5No page should be more than three clicks from home.
