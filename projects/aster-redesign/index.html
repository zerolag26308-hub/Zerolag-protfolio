01 · AuditWhat was getting in the way

A walkthrough of the storefront on desktop and a 390px phone, scored against Nielsen's heuristics and WCAG 2.2 AA. Severity reflects how often shoppers hit the issue and how much it blocks them.

Show 10 findings
  • HighA-01

    Checkout is spread across five screens

    Heuristic
    Flexibility and efficiency of use
    Where
    Checkout, every step

    Fix. Collapse to two steps: details and delivery together, then payment and review on one screen with the summary always visible.

  • HighA-02

    Guest checkout hidden under account creation

    Heuristic
    User control and freedom
    Where
    Checkout step 1

    Fix. Start with an email field only. Offer to save an account on the confirmation page, after the order is placed.

  • HighA-03

    Shipping cost appears only at the payment step

    Heuristic
    Visibility of system status
    Where
    Cart and checkout

    Fix. Show delivery options with prices on the product page and in the cart drawer, and keep the running total in the summary.

  • MediumA-04

    Fabric and leg options give no stock or lead-time state

    Heuristic
    Visibility of system status
    Where
    Product page

    Fix. Each option shows "ready to ship" or "made to order, 5 weeks", and the add button repeats the chosen lead time.

  • MediumA-05

    Filter and sort state lost on the way back

    Heuristic
    Recognition rather than recall
    Where
    Shop grid to product and back

    Fix. Keep category and sort in the URL and restore the scroll position, so the back button returns shoppers to where they were.

  • MediumA-06

    Product cards need a page visit to add anything

    Heuristic
    Flexibility and efficiency of use
    Where
    Shop grid

    Fix. Quick add on the card for single-variant goods; for sized or upholstered items the same button opens the option sheet.

  • MediumA-07

    Caption grey falls below the contrast minimum

    Heuristic
    WCAG 1.4.3 contrast (minimum)
    Where
    Lead times, hints, card captions

    Fix. Replace #8A9490 on stone (2.6:1) with #56605B (5.5:1). Lead times move from caption grey to the regular ink.

  • LowA-08

    Cart count disappears inside the mobile menu

    Heuristic
    Visibility of system status
    Where
    Navigation under 860px

    Fix. The cart icon and badge stay in the header on every width. Only the category links collapse into the menu.

  • LowA-09

    Form errors listed at the top, after submit

    Heuristic
    Help users recognise and recover from errors
    Where
    Checkout forms

    Fix. Validate on blur, print the message under the field it belongs to and move focus to the first problem.

  • LowA-10

    Made-to-order lead time buried in the description

    Heuristic
    Match between system and the real world
    Where
    Product page, cart line

    Fix. Lead time sits beside the add button and repeats on the cart line and in the order summary, as a date range, not a number of weeks.
