    Production notes

    A reel is a studio's sketchbook: eight five-second studies, each isolating one technique we reach for on client work. Logo reveals, product micro-interactions, type that behaves like a material, a particle field, liquid shapes, a tile transition, an odometer counter and a kinetic end card.

    Every study is parametric. The particle field is seeded, so it replays identically on every pass and scrubs to any frame; the liquid shapes are polar harmonics rather than keyframes; the odometer rolls digit by digit from a single value. Cuts land exactly on the five-second marks so the reel can be re-ordered or trimmed without re-timing.
