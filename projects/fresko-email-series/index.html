04 / 04
Sending rules

      
The flow only works if it knows when to stay quiet. These rules sit above every journey.

    

    

      
Timing
  • Nothing sends between 10pm and 8am in the person's own time zone
  • Food emails land at 11am or 5pm, when people decide what to eat
  • Abandoned cart waits 45 minutes, long enough to finish checking out on their own

      
Frequency
  • At most one lifecycle email per day and three per week, per person
  • Abandoned cart fires once in any seven days, no matter how many carts
  • A placed order cancels any pending nudge or win-back in the queue

      
Consent and exits
  • Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe and a link to notification settings in the app
  • Unsubscribing from marketing keeps order receipts and delivery updates
  • Anyone who deletes their account leaves every journey the same minute
