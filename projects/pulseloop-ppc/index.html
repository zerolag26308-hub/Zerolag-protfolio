Pulseloop

Digital marketing · Paid acquisition

Three campaigns, twelve ads and two landing pages, built to be switched on

Pulseloop is a running and habit app. This is the paid plan to grow it: a campaign structure that keeps intent separate from reach, ads built for each placement, a landing page test, the audiences each campaign talks to, and a planner that turns one monthly number into daily budgets.

01 / 05

Campaign structure

Three campaigns with one job each. Search catches people already looking, app install campaigns find people like them, and retargeting talks only to people who have met Pulseloop before. Open any ad group to see its keywords or assets.

02 / 05

Twelve ad variants

Four for each placement, each with a different angle so the test tells us something. Everything is drawn in HTML, CSS and SVG at the real placement proportions.

03 / 05

Landing page test

Two pages with the same offer and one difference: A explains the plan, B asks the visitor to pick a goal first. Both are working pages; try the goal picker in B.
