Cedarview is a neighborhood clinic in Wrenfield with ten clinicians under one roof. Visits start close to on time, run long enough for your questions, and a real person picks up the phone.

        Insurance and paying

        We're in network with these plans:

        • Larkfield Mutual (all PPO plans)
        • Stonebrook Health Plan
        • Oakline Community Cover
        • Fairhaven Employee Benefits
        • Wrenfield County Medical Assistance

        Not on the list? Give us a call. We can often still bill your plan as out of network.

        Payment is due on the day. Every service and price is listed in the services section.

        We collect your co-pay at check-in and bill your plan for the rest. Any balance arrives as a plain itemized statement within about 30 days, and our billing desk is on (555) 555-0144, weekdays from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm.

        Yes. Balances over $150 can be split across up to six interest-free monthly payments, and our sliding-scale program is open to Wrenfield County households. Ask at the front desk and we'll go through the form with you.

        What to expect at your visit

        • A photo ID, and your insurance card if you have one
        • A list of any medicines you take
        • Any questions you'd like to cover, written down if that helps
        • Immunization records for a child's first visit

        Fifteen minutes before a first visit, so there's time for the new-patient form. Five minutes is plenty after that.

        Check in at the front desk. A medical assistant will bring you through, confirm your details and take any routine measurements. Then you'll have your time with your clinician, who will tell you about any next steps before you leave.

        Of course. Family, a friend or a carer are welcome in the room, and you can ask for a chaperone at any visit. Children under 16 should attend with a parent or guardian.

        The whole clinic is step-free, with an elevator and a wide-door restroom on each floor. Phone interpreters are free for any language, and a sign language interpreter can be arranged with 48 hours' notice.

        Please let us know at least 24 hours ahead so someone else can have the time. Late cancellations carry a $25 fee, and we waive the first one without question.
