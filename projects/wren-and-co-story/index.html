Production notes

Wren & Co. makes small-run furniture and objects, so the film is paced like the label: slow pushes and drifts across still photography, chapter cards set in a light serif, and a lot of air. Nothing cuts faster than two seconds until the closing montage.

The grade is built as a stack rather than a single filter: a warm multiply wash, a lift in the shadows, a radial vignette and a 24 fps grain pass that re-seeds every frame. The 2.20:1 letterbox sits inside a 16:9 master so the piece plays on any screen without a crop.
